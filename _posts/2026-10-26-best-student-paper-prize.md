We are delighted to announce that the first MESS Best Student Paper Prize goes to **Mario Tamayo Sanz** (UC3M) for the paper *"From Institutions to Faces? Strategic Personalization in the Digital Presence of the European Commission"*. The prize was awarded at the 2026 MESS Conference on October 22–23 at CSIC, following the plenary talk by Aina Gallego.

We would also like to congratulate **Héctor Martínez Pérez** (UC3M), who received an honorable mention for the paper *"When algorithms replace parties: AI surveillance and elite power-sharing in autocracies"*.

The prize recognizes the best paper presented at the conference by a doctoral student enrolled at a Madrid university. Many thanks to the prize committee, Covadonga Meseguer, Aina Gallego, and Catherine De Vries, and to all PhD students who presented their work this year.
