The program for the third annual MESS conference on October 22–23 at the Institute of Public Goods and Policies (CSIC) is out! We have 22 panels and more than 80 papers across political science, sociology, economics and economic history, a plenary talk by Aina Gallego (University of Barcelona) on *"The Political Economy of the Post-Knowledge Economy"*, and the first-ever MESS Best PhD Student Paper Prize. Here is the [conference program](/assets/img/MESS2026_Program.pdf) and the [schedule by room](/assets/img/MESS2026_Schedule_by_Room.pdf). Even if you are not presenting, you are very welcome to attend. You can find more information about the conference, including how to sign up, [here](/mess2026).
