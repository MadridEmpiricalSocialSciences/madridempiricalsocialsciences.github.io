The second annual conference of the Madrid Empirical Social Sciences Network took place at IE Tower in October 2025. Building on the success of our inaugural meeting in 2024, it brought together scholars from political science, sociology, economics, and neighboring social sciences for two days of lively discussions and networking. Thank you to everyone who presented, discussed, and attended!

<div class="mess-facts" markdown="1">
- **Date:** October 24–25, 2025
- **Location:** IE Tower, P.º de la Castellana, 259, Madrid
- **Guest speaker:** Stuart J. Turnbull-Dugarte (University of Southampton)
- **Program:** [Conference program (pdf)](/assets/img/MESS2025-Program.pdf)
</div>
