The third annual conference of the Madrid Empirical Social Sciences Network took place at the Institute of Public Goods and Policies (CSIC) in October 2026. Building on the momentum of our previous meetings, it brought together researchers from political science, sociology, economics and economic history for two days of stimulating debate and networking, with 22 panels and more than 80 papers. Thank you to everyone who presented, discussed, chaired, and attended!

<div class="mess-facts" markdown="1">
- **Date:** October 22–23, 2026
- **Location:** Institute of Public Goods and Policies (IPP-CSIC), C/ Albasanz 26–28, 28037 Madrid
- **Guest speaker:** Aina Gallego (University of Barcelona)
- **Program:** [Full program (pdf)](/assets/img/MESS2026_Program.pdf) · [Schedule by room (pdf)](/assets/img/MESS2026_Schedule_by_Room.pdf)
</div>

## Best Student Paper Prize

This year, the MESS network awarded its first **Best Student Paper Prize** for the best paper presented by a doctoral student enrolled at a Madrid university. Congratulations to the winner and the runner-up:

- **Winner:** Mario Tamayo Sanz (UC3M), *"From Institutions to Faces? Strategic Personalization in the Digital Presence of the European Commission"*
- **Honorable mention:** Héctor Martínez Pérez (UC3M), *"When algorithms replace parties: AI surveillance and elite power-sharing in autocracies"*

Many thanks to the prize committee, Covadonga Meseguer, Aina Gallego, and Catherine De Vries.

## Keynote

**Aina Gallego** (University of Barcelona) delivered the plenary talk *"The Political Economy of the Post-Knowledge Economy"* on Thursday, October 22, followed by the announcement of the Best Student Paper Prize.
