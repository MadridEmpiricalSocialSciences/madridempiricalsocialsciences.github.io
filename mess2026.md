The Madrid Empirical Social Sciences network is thrilled to welcome you to its third annual conference! Building on the momentum of our previous meetings, we once again bring together researchers from political science, sociology, and the wider social sciences in and around Madrid for two days of stimulating debate and networking. This year's program features 22 panels and more than 80 papers across political science, sociology, economics and economic history.

## Attending the Conference

All members of the MESS community are welcome to attend whichever panels they like. Presenting or discussing is not required to join! To help us plan room capacity, please sign up using [this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeWriREZNxCNBS52K7qmfYSHGsbYGvggBOvVzJwBDQm6vj4lg/viewform). The registration deadline was **October 14, 2026**.

## At the Venue

- Please **arrive at least 15 minutes before your panel** so that sessions can start on time.
- Coffee will be provided during the morning breaks in room 0E19 (Sala de Recepciones). Lunch is not provided: participants are free to have lunch on their own.

## Information for Paper Presenters

- Please **upload your paper by October 16** to our [shared Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1mf94QDihY-T-ibhX9VvNQu9nRR6gpNUS?usp=drive_link), so discussants can read it before the conference. Start the file name with your panel number and surname (e.g., "Panel 7_Surname_Paper.pdf").
- We highly encourage you to upload your slides to the same folder ahead of your session, using the same naming.
- Your presentation should be no longer than **15 minutes in panels with four papers** and **20 minutes in panels with three papers** to leave enough time for feedback and discussion.

## Information for Discussants

- We have assigned **two discussants per panel**, each discussing two papers, and **one discussant in panels with three papers**. Each paper's discussant is listed under it in the [full program](/assets/img/MESS2026_Program.pdf).
- Your comments should be no longer than **5 minutes per paper** to leave enough time for a Q&A session with the audience.

## Information for Chairs

- Chairs keep time and moderate the Q&A. Please make sure every presenter gets their full slot.

## Keynote

**Aina Gallego** (University of Barcelona) will deliver the plenary talk *"The Political Economy of the Post-Knowledge Economy"* on Thursday, October 22, 17:00–18:45 in the Plenary Room.

## Best PhD Student Paper Prize

To promote excellence in the next generation of scholars, the MESS network is inaugurating the **Best PhD Student Paper Prize** this year. A dedicated academic committee will award it to the best paper presented by a doctoral student enrolled in a doctoral program at a Madrid university. The prize will be presented after the plenary talk on Thursday.
